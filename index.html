Frequently Asked Questions

How does Pezeex find cheaper alternatives?

Pezeex compares the product name or link you submit against similar options offered by trusted regional and international online retailers, highlighting lower-priced alternatives with comparable specifications.

Is Pezeex free to use?

Yes. Pezeex is completely free with up to 25 searches per day. We may earn a small affiliate commission on qualifying clicks or purchases at no extra cost to you. Always verify final prices and shipping terms on the merchant website.
